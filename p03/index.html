Brand Identity

I am attempting to situate my ethos as a game designer and developer steeped in a love for games and all of the things that emerge from them. Gameplay, narrative, and community can all emerge from game systems. As part of this, I am taking visual inspiration for my portfolio from classic tabletop roleplaying game rulebooks and art, as those games showcased how utilizing game systems can create gameplay more involved than the rules alone would suggest and stories that came from playing the game itself. By using this inspiration I hope to convey a deep love and understanding of the medium, as well as stand out from other sites that may hew closer to clean and modern visual styles.

Possible Fonts

League Spartan would make a good choice for regular text, as it is simple and readable, while also bearing a resemblance to the font used within the rulebook of the first edition of Dungeons and Dragons, which ties it into the overall aesthetic theme.

QuentinCaps could be used for important headers and titles, as it is eye catching and impressive. It is, or at least is similar to, the font used for the cover page of the first Dungeons and Dragons rulebook, tying it to the aesthetic in the same way as League Spartan. However, it is somewhat busy, so avoiding visual clutter will be important. It may also clash with other aspects of the aesthetic, so it may need to be replaced.
